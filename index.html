A Letter From My Heart

Dear Dad

Dear Dad,

I have been thinking about my second semester result, and I know that I did not perform as well as I should have. I am truly sorry.

I know how much you have sacrificed for my education and how much you want to see me succeed. I understand why you are disappointed in me, and I accept responsibility for my performance.

I am not writing this letter to make excuses. I know I could have prepared better, studied harder, and managed my time more wisely.

Please Don't Give Up On Me

Dad, I am asking you to please give me another opportunity to prove myself.

I understand that my result has made you question whether I am ready to continue, but I want you to know that I have learned from this experience.

I don't want this result to be the reason I lose the opportunity to complete my education. I want to continue my studies and show you that I can become better.

What I Will Do Differently

  • I will take my studies more seriously.
  • I will create a proper study timetable and follow it.
  • I will start preparing for examinations much earlier.
  • I will ask for help whenever I don't understand a topic.
  • I will practice more questions and revise consistently.
  • I will make sure my actions show you that I am serious.

Dad, I know that saying "I'm sorry" cannot change my result. I also know that promises mean very little without action. That is why I want my improvement to speak for me.

Please give me one more chance.

I am grateful for everything you have done for me and everything you continue to do for me. I don't take your sacrifices for granted.

I am sorry for disappointing you, Dad. I will learn from this mistake, work harder, and do everything I can to make you proud.

With love, respect, and sincerity,

Your Son

— Jesutooto
